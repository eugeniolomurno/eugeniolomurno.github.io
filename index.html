About

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the AIRLab group at the Department of Electronics, Information and Bioengineering (DEIB), Politecnico di Milano, where I completed my PhD in Information Technology cum laude in 2025. Today my research develops along three connected directions:

  • Privacy through synthetic data and their secure sharing — training and exchanging models without ever exposing the people behind the data.
  • Synthetic data that surpass real data — generating data that are not merely a safe substitute, but more useful and informative for the AI models that learn from them.
  • A European, Human-Centered vision of AI — advancing it as a constructive counterpoint to the prevailing American and Chinese approaches.

My research has been carried out within the H2020 ESSENCE and AI-SPRINT projects and the PNRR FAIR project, and continues today — in the Lombardy-funded PREDICT project — with cutting-edge generative techniques for synthesising medical-imaging data of rare tumours. I am also part of the SEL (Smart Eyewear Lab), a joint research centre of EssilorLuxottica and Politecnico di Milano devoted to next-generation smart eyewear.
